## Result and scope

The manuscript studies the zero-field Gaussian pure Ising p-spin model at its static critical inverse temperature, for every fixed integer p ≥ 3. The annealed-normalized partition function converges in probability to 1/2. With the deterministic planted-band centering defined in the paper, its fluctuations have scale N^(-1/2) and a spectrally positive stable component of index one.

The limiting law also has an independent Gaussian component for p = 3 in the distinct-index models, and for p = 3, 4 in the ordered-tensor model. The exact finite-size covariance convention and its corresponding centering are retained. Total log-partition-function fluctuations have scale N^(-1/2); free energy per spin has scale N^(-3/2). A median-centered formulation is also proved.

The theorem concerns weak convergence. It does not give an expectation-centered limiting law or an asymptotic equivalent for the variance.

## Review status

The supplied manuscript underwent independent AI reviews of the auxiliary-field estimates, planted-band and local-limit arguments, and the exceptional three- and four-spin corrections, followed by a separate review of the dependencies between these arguments. These checks identified no substantive mathematical errors or gaps in the stated proofs. The scalar integral bounds and the 7-, 17-, and 26-dimensional matrix certificates were independently reconstructed and checked, using directed decimal bounds and exact rational arithmetic.

This is an AI audit, not a Lean formalization or a guarantee of complete correctness. Readers should check the arguments independently. The manuscript refers to companion certificate programs; the original programs were not supplied with this PDF and are not included in this folder. The numerical audit used the algorithms and error bounds printed in the manuscript.
