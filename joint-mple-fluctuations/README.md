## Result and scope

The manuscript treats joint estimation of inverse temperature and the natural external-field coefficient from one SK configuration with the Gaussian coupling matrix observed. At every fixed positive inverse temperature and zero true field, it gives conditional Gaussian fluctuations, estimator existence and uniqueness with high probability, and fitted-pair covariance estimation. Both parameters are fitted.

The deterministic normalizers may depend on system size; convergence to a fixed covariance is not assumed. Arbitrary nonzero true fields and a quantitative multivariate approximation rate are not claimed. The joint convergence uses the bounded Wasserstein distance W_{1,rho}, with rho(x,y)=min(||x-y||,2).

The literature discussion includes Chen–Sen–Wu's *Joint parameter estimations for spin glasses*, Mukherjee–Sen–Wu's *Joint parameters estimation in cubic tensor model*, and earlier classical, Curie–Weiss, dense-graph and joint pseudolikelihood fluctuation results.
