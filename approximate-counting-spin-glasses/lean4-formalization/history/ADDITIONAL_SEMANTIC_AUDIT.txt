RANDOM-COEFFICIENT BRIDGE
- Parseval applies to the actual difference of two finite sign polynomials. Squared coefficient
  differences are exactly the error mass; no unbiasedness or coefficientwise independence is
  assumed or needed. The retained-family result uses literal membership filtering.
- The integrated statement permits an arbitrary outer measure and assumes integrability of
  each squared coefficient difference. This suffices for finite-sum integration; separate
  measurability/integrability of c and d is unnecessary when their difference cancels.
- Coefficients depend on omega, not on the inner sign variable eta. Consequently the statement
  describes an iterated average with fresh uniform signs. It does not establish that an
  arbitrary symmetric disorder model, or sign-dependent algorithm, has this representation.
  The module documentation states that remaining obligation correctly.
