1. Ordered SK normalization
The Hamiltonian uses 1/sqrt(2N), with the full ordered sum including diagonal
entries. Each off-diagonal unordered coefficient is (G_ij+G_ji)/sqrt(2), and the
main theorem uses beta/sqrt(N). Their product is beta*(G_ij+G_ji)/sqrt(2N), so both
orientations have precisely the original coefficient. The diagonal is
C_G=(sum_i G_ii)/sqrt(2N); spinSign_sq=1 makes this spin-independent.
The probability-normalized partition is exactly
2^(-N)*exp(beta*C_G)*Z_counting. Thus its log is
log Z_counting - N*log 2 + beta*C_G. The signs and factors in finish_value and
logarithmic_error_preserved agree. The additive correction cancels pathwise in
the absolute error; no independence of the diagonal from transformed couplings is
needed for this argument. N>0 is explicitly assumed where denominators are used;
all main SK accuracy theorems already require N>=2.

2. Gaussian law and mutual independence
coordinate_injective sends Upper(N) x Bool to distinct ordered off-diagonal
positions. This prevents orientation collisions and prevents sharing an original
Gaussian between different unordered pairs. grouped_law is a complete product
measure identity, rather than a pairwise-independence claim. mix_law adds two
independent variance-one Gaussian variables and divides by sqrt(2), so the
resulting variance is 2/(sqrt(2))^2=1. transformed_joint_law gives the full joint
product law, and transformed_independent is mutual independence.
The new upperEdge/edgeEquiv bridge is a bijection onto precisely the two-element
physical edge type: injectivity uses the upper-triangle order to remove swapping,
and surjectivity uses upperPairs_image. physical_joint_law and
physical_joint_law_with_coins therefore put the exact coupled inputs under the
same iid physical-edge law as PhysicalInput.sk_end_to_end. Gaussian symmetry,
unit second moment, and finite fourth moment are independently proved from
Mathlib's Gaussian measure results. No unproved Gaussian-law premise remains.

3. Preprocessing and output correction
pairSchedule is a concrete row-by-row schedule of length N^2, with a nodup proof.
pairLoop tests the strict upper-triangle condition before forming a transformed
value. prepare_entry identifies every stored value with the required coupling;
prepare_keys_nodup proves that no transformed pair is stored twice. The diagonal
is computed separately and sqrt(2) and sqrt(2N) are formed once.
The operation proofs charge arithmetic, comparisons, schedule generation and
storage. prepare_operations gives <=30N^2, finish_operations is exactly6, and
transformation_operations gives <=36N^2 for N>0. These are genuine polynomial
preprocessing bounds in the stated abstract model. The output correction is
numerically identical to the paper and finish_error preserves the original error.

4. Conditional fixed-instance success
conditional_markov assumes an actual product of two probability measures and a
measurable joint failure event. Its conditionalFailure is the color-section
probability for a fixed disorder input, with the correct product-coordinate
orientation. The proof uses the product-measure section integral, then Markov at
sqrt(zeta). For zeta>0, the final division is justified by sqrt(zeta)>0.
For zeta=0, the proof separately obtains zero total failure measure, hence zero
section failure almost everywhere, and zero measure of the set where conditional
failure is strictly positive. It does not divide by zero or discard this case.
The strict bad-event inequality (>sqrt(zeta)) means the complement has conditional
failure <=sqrt(zeta), as in the manuscript. There is deliberately no assertion for
every adversarial fixed coupling array. zeta may exceed1; the theorem remains
valid, though then uninformative. The specialized result uses zeta=delta+tau and
retains tau rather than silently eliminating the disorder tail remainder.
